# RTI Online redesign decisions

## Citizen problems addressed

Citizens arrive to decide whether RTI is appropriate, check whether information is already public, identify the record-holding authority, file precisely, track the statutory clock, appeal an inadequate response, or resolve an uncertain payment. The interface therefore presents these as explicit tasks instead of treating filing as the only entry point.

## RTI Online elements preserved

The redesign retains the centred portal frame, blue institutional masthead, RTI Online name, Government of India and Department of Personnel & Training ownership text, language control, public-authority link, compact service navigation, jurisdiction warning, help access, and institutional footer. These familiar signals support recognition and trust. A persistent high-contrast prototype disclosure prevents the independent concept from being mistaken for the official service.

The established labels—Submit Request, Submit First Appeal, View Status, View History, Login, FAQ, Contact Us, and Payment Reconciliation—remain visible and route to the repository's working experiences.

## FOIA.gov patterns adapted

The homepage adapts a task-first journey: understand the access-to-information mechanism, search already-published information, find the likely record holder, then file. Plain-language descriptions explain the purpose and next step of every task. Key facts are scannable rather than buried in prose. No source code, brand asset, or proprietary visual from FOIA.gov is used.

## Elements removed or reduced

The oversized marketing hero, promotional claims about speed, large decorative whitespace, heavy shadows, and startup-style cards were reduced. They delayed access to core services and made the portal feel like a campaign site. Decorative imagery and invented performance statistics are deliberately absent.

## Final information hierarchy

1. Government identity and explicit independent-prototype boundary.
2. Compact primary and information navigation.
3. One-sentence service explanation and an RTI-versus-grievance prompt.
4. Unmissable Central-versus-State jurisdiction warning.
5. Filing, appeal, status, history, and payment actions.
6. Understand → Search → Find → File guided journey.
7. Fee, exemption, response-period, tracking, and appeal facts.
8. Help desk and institutional footer.

This order follows the decisions a citizen must make, prevents common misrouting and duplicate-payment errors, and keeps every existing workflow reachable.

## Evidence and constraints

The official RTI Online hierarchy and supplied reference capture informed the institutional shell and labels. The requested official sites could not be fetched in the execution environment because its outbound proxy returned HTTP 403, so no claims were added beyond the repository's existing RTI guidance. Important legal and fee language remains qualified (for example, “usual” response period and eligible BPL applicants) and the interface continues to identify all interactions and records as synthetic.
